abstract: >-
  Why do AI agents succeed in digital environments yet struggle with autonomous scientific discovery? This review proposes three stages of capability externalization: reasoning, execution, and world interaction. Using materials discovery as a testbed, it identifies environmental verifiability as a central constraint on sustained autonomy: actions must yield timely, attributable, reproducible, and affordable evidence. Physical verification infrastructure is therefore essential to connecting automated workflows with hypothesis revision and reproducible discovery.
