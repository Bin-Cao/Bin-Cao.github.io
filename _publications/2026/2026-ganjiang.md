abstract: >-
  Ganjiang integrates XMatcher, XQueryer, XDecomposer, and WPEM to identify phases, separate mixtures, and refine structures from powder diffraction. It diagnoses failures and validates revised analytical skills before reuse, without retraining the language model. On DeltaXRDbench, it outperforms the evaluated methods for single- and multiphase identification on simulated and experimental data, linking physical evidence with expertise that transfers to new samples.
